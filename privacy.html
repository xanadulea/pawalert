Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Paw Alert is built by Corin Studios s.p. ("we," "us," "our"), a company registered in Slovenia. This policy explains what information Paw Alert collects, why, and what control you have over it.

What we collect

  • Account info — your name and email address when you register.
  • Location — only when you choose to post a sighting or use the map. You control how precisely it's shared: exact location, neighborhood-level only, or shared just with someone who messages you about a specific post.
  • Photos — images you upload when reporting a lost, found, urgent, or happy pet post.
  • Messages — chat messages you send to other users through the app.
  • Device notification token — used only to deliver push notifications to your device. You can turn notifications off entirely, or mute individual conversations, at any time inside the app.

Why we collect it

Every piece of information above exists to make one thing work: helping people report and find lost or found animals, and letting them communicate about it safely. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising — Paw Alert has no ads.

Who can see what

  • Posts you make (photos, description, chosen location precision) are visible to other users on the map and browse screens, since that's the whole point of reporting a sighting.
  • Messages you send are only visible to you and the person you're messaging.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

Where your data is stored

Paw Alert is built on Google Firebase, and your data is stored on Google Cloud's infrastructure with industry-standard security. We do not run our own separate servers holding your personal data.

Your rights

Since Corin Studios s.p. is based in Slovenia, an EU member state, you have rights under GDPR regardless of where you live, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

You can delete your account directly inside the app at any time (Settings → Delete Account), which removes your profile and posts. For any other request, or questions about this policy, contact us at pawandalert@gmail.com.

Children

Paw Alert is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to how we handle your data, we'll update this page and change the "last updated" date above.
